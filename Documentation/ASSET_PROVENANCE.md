# Academic lab asset provenance

Verified 25 September 2026.

## Imported

**Kenney Furniture Kit** — https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit

- Licence: CC0; original licence retained at `Assets/MoleculeLab/Art/KenneyFurniture/License.txt`.
- Selected meshes: computerScreen, computerKeyboard, books, chairDesk, plantSmall1.
- Source: the official Kenney ZIP linked from that page.
- Changes: removed unused OBJ material references; assigned shared project URP materials; normalized scale for scene placement.
- No third-party scripts, shaders, sample scenes or unused models imported.

## Researched, not imported

**Free Laboratory Pack**, StormBringer Studios — https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/tools/free-laboratory-pack-123782

The listing is free under the Unity Asset Store EULA and originates from Unity 2017.4. Its actual meshes/materials have not been imported or verified in this project. To avoid making delivery depend on account acquisition and old shaders, custom geometry supplies the bottles, flasks and instrument enclosure instead.

## Original project content

Room shell, worktable, rack, baskets, socket layout, signs, posters, reagent archive, instrument enclosure and ventilation detail are generated project geometry. Audio is synthesized locally. The report diagrams are original editable TikZ drawings. The provided evaluation slides are cited as a course source and are not bundled in the report ZIP.

Neither the selected meshes nor the use of opaque materials certifies Quest performance. Profiling remains a hardware acceptance task.
