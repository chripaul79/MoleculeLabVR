\section*{Animation and audio}
Animation is reserved for state changes: seed transfer, bonding preview, a short snap, ethane torsion, reversible deformation and reaction rearrangement. There is no ambient object motion competing for attention. During reaction, the status text explains that the system controls the inputs; reset remains the escape route.

Quiet ventilation supplies room ambience. A brief ready tone, bond tone and completion tone are distinguishable but restrained. Sound can be muted; reduced effects remove arc motion while retaining the release state. Text and geometry convey every essential outcome without audio.

\section*{Assets and performance choices}
Selected computer, keyboard, books, chair and plant meshes come from Kenney's CC0 Furniture Kit \cite{kenney}. Their materials are replaced to match the room. Custom geometry supplies the shell, shelf, baskets, reagent bottles, glassware and instrument enclosure. The Free Laboratory Pack was researched as an optional source \cite{freelab}; it is not required by the delivered scene. No third-party meshes are included in this report archive.

Use opaque materials, shared materials, simple static props and a bounded number of line effects. Avoid refractive glass, extra realtime effect lights and large transparent particle systems. Low file size and low polygon counts alone do not prove Quest performance.
