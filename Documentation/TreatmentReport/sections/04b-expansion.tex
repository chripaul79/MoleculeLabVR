\section*{Direct molecules, ray-operated menus}
\textbf{Implemented interaction design.} Nearby thumb--index pinches acquire atoms and completed molecules. Hand rays operate menus only; fingertip menu presses and distant hand-ray object grabs are disabled. One pinch belongs to one target class until released. A hand holding an object cannot also operate a menu. Controllers retain grip grabs and ray/trigger selection.

The board has Build, Inspect, React and Settings pages, with four molecule choices per page. Buttons are at least 5 cm high with visible hover and pressed states. Height and forward/back controls support workstation reach. These choices address consistency, affordance, error prevention and recovery; effectiveness still requires headset observation.

\section*{Organic chemistry expansion}
The added structures are ethene, ethyne, methanol, ethanol, carbon dioxide and benzene. Idealized sp, sp$^2$ and sp$^3$ geometry is distinguished from measured molecular dimensions. Benzene is planar with equivalent C--C bonds and a delocalization circle for six $\pi$ electrons. Internal Kekul\'e bookkeeping is not presented as alternating physical bond lengths. Ring closure connects all required occupied neighbours once. Aromatic and multiple bonds do not use the ethane twist tool \cite{benzene}.

Oxygen's triplet ground state is identified; an O=O drawing alone is not a complete electronic model. Ethane homolysis yields two methyl radicals with an unpaired electron each. The energy tool is illustrative, not a measurement of bond dissociation energy.
